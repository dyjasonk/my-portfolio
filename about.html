Biography

Jason K. Dy, SJ

Jason Dy, SJ (b. 1977, Philippines) is a Jesuit priest, Filipino contemporary artist, and independent curator who is on faculty leave at the Fine Arts Department, Ateneo de Manila University. Currently, he is pursuing a PhD in Art at the London Metropolitan University, exploring contemporary religious art in public spheres through archival and relational approaches.
